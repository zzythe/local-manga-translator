Local Manga Translator

A browser extension that translates manga, manhwa and manhua in place, offline.

Chrome Edge Firefox

Runs locally

Pages are read by a vision model in Ollama on your machine. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account.

Works on most readers

Long strips, page-by-page readers and galleries. Turn it on for a tab, or have a site translated automatically.

Reads ahead

The next few pages are translated while you read the current one. Pages you've seen come back instantly.

Re-lettered

The original text is removed from each bubble and the translation is set to fit its shape.

Sound effects

Captions, narration and text drawn on the art are translated as well as speech bubbles.

15 languages

From Japanese, Korean, Chinese, English, Spanish or Portuguese into English and 14 others.

FAQ

What do I need?

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera or Firefox, and Ollama on Windows, macOS or Linux. Models take 2 to 7 GB of disk space. A GPU with 6 GB of VRAM or more translates a page in about 10 seconds; on the CPU it can take a minute or more.

How do I set it up?

Install the extension and a setup page opens. It links the Ollama installer for your system and downloads the model once Ollama is running.

Does it cost anything?

30 new pages a day are free, and images from long strips count as half a page. Pro removes the limit for a one-time $6.99. The translation runs on your machine either way.

Why does it need access to all websites?

Readers load pages from many different image hosts, and the extension has to download the image to translate it. It does nothing on a site until you turn it on there.

How good are the translations?

They're machine translations. Clean scans with white speech bubbles work best.

Where do I report a bug?

On GitHub.
