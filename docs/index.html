Questions

What do I need?

A Windows 10/11, macOS or Linux computer with about 4 to 8 GB of free disk space. A graphics card with 6 GB or more of memory translates a page in seconds; without one it works, but slowly. Chrome, Edge, Brave, Vivaldi, Opera or Firefox.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The extension downloads the page image from the site you're reading, exactly as your browser already does, and sends it to Ollama on your own computer. It has no analytics and no server of its own. Details in the privacy policy.

Why does it ask to read and change data on all websites?

Comic readers load pages from many different image servers, so the extension needs permission to download those images and to put the translated page in place. It does nothing on a site until you turn it on there.

How good are the translations?

Good enough to follow the story comfortably, but they're machine translations. Clean scans with white speech bubbles work best; text drawn over busy artwork is sometimes left as it is rather than damaging the art.

Does Pro run anything in the cloud?

No. Pro only removes the daily limit; the translation still runs on your PC. Payments are handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe.

Can I see the code?

Yes. The source is on GitHub so you can check what it does. You may build it for your own use.
