Local Manga Translator

Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Local Manga Translator ("the extension") is made by zzythe. It is designed so that what you read stays on your computer. This policy explains exactly what the extension handles and where it goes.

Summary

  • The extension has no server of its own and no analytics or tracking.
  • Comic pages are translated on your computer by Ollama, a program you install yourself. They are not sent to the developer or to any online translation service.
  • If you buy Pro, the payment is handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe, who receive your email address and payment details. The developer receives your email address and payment status, never your card details.

What the extension does with web pages

When you turn the translator on for a page, it finds the comic images on that page and downloads them from the website that serves them, just as your browser does when it shows them. It sends your cookies and the page address to that website with the request, because many comic sites refuse image downloads without them. The images are sent to Ollama at 127.0.0.1, on your own computer, for reading and translation. Translated pages are kept in memory only and never saved, so a page you go back to or reload shows again at once. They are dropped when you close the tab or the browser. A translated page is saved to your computer only when you click Save image on it.

On readers that show one page per address, the extension also downloads the next page of the site, with the current page as referrer, to translate its image ahead. It does this one page at a time and only while the translator is on.

The extension does nothing on a website until you turn it on there, either with the toolbar button, the right-click menu or the keyboard shortcut, or by setting it to translate that site automatically. Once you turn it on, it stays on for that site, in other tabs too, until you close it with the X button or close the browser. This is kept in memory only.

What is stored

In your browser's extension storage, on your computer only: your settings, the sites you chose to translate automatically, the names you entered for each site, how many pages you translated today and in total for the free plan's daily limit, and whether Pro is active. So that a page shown again isn't counted twice, it also keeps a short code for each page counted today, made from the image address and a random value that changes every day. The codes are deleted the next day. None of this is sent to the developer.

Payments (Pro)

Pro is sold through ExtensionPay, which uses Stripe to process payments. When you check whether Pro is active, or buy or restore it, the extension contacts ExtensionPay with an anonymous identifier for your installation. If you pay or log in, ExtensionPay and Stripe receive the email address and payment details you enter. Their privacy policies apply: ExtensionPay, Stripe.

Downloads during setup

The setup page links to Ollama's official download and asks Ollama to download a translation model from Ollama's model library. Those downloads come from Ollama's servers under Ollama's terms.

The extension also downloads two small models from Hugging Face once (72 MB in all: one finds speech bubbles, one repaints the artwork under sound effects) and keeps them in the browser. Nothing from your pages is sent with those requests.

What the extension never does

  • It doesn't sell, share or transfer your data, and doesn't use it for advertising, credit or any purpose unrelated to translating the pages you ask it to.
  • It doesn't read your browsing history or the content of pages you haven't turned it on for.
  • It doesn't run code downloaded from the internet.

Children

The extension is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: open an issue at github.com/zzythe/local-manga-translator.
