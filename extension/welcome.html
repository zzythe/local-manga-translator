Questions

Is anything uploaded?

No. Pages are read by the model on your computer through Ollama. The extension only connects to the comic site you're reading, to Ollama on your PC, to Hugging Face once to download two small helper models, and to ExtensionPay if you buy Pro.

Why does it need access to all websites?

Manga readers load their pages from many different image servers. To translate a page, the extension has to download the same image you're looking at. It does nothing on a site until you turn it on there.

It's slow

Speed depends on your graphics card. Try the Light model, close games and other heavy programs, and keep Ollama up to date. On computers without a graphics card a page can take a minute or more.

Which languages?

From Japanese, Korean, Chinese, English, Spanish and Portuguese into English and 14 other languages. Choose them with the sliders button on the translator.

How do I remove everything?

Remove the extension from your browser, then uninstall Ollama from Windows Settings → Apps, or drag it to the Trash on a Mac. On Windows that also deletes the model.
